# SpeciMap field guide

## Before you leave (with signal)

1. Sign in to SpeciMap at home — sign-in needs email access, the field does
   not.
2. **Install the app**: open SpeciMap in your browser → Share → "Add to Home
   Screen" (iOS) or "Install app" (Android). This protects your queued
   records from browser storage cleanup and makes tag scans open instantly.
3. Optionally, pan around your field site on the map screen once — viewed
   map tiles are cached for offline use.
4. Pack printed tags, vials, and pencils.

## In the field (no signal needed)

1. Drop a tag in the vial (or under the cap) with the specimen.
2. Scan the tag's QR with your phone camera → SpeciMap opens.
3. Photograph the specimen **with the tag visible in frame**. Follow the
   focus indicator: green = sharp. If it stays red at close range, move
   *back* a little — phone lenses can't focus very close.
4. Confirm the tag number matches, confirm the pin on the map (drag it if
   the GPS is off), add a note if useful, save.
5. The record is stored on your phone: "Queued — will upload when online."

## Back home

Open the app while connected — queued records upload automatically (watch
the pending counter on the home screen). iPhones don't sync in the
background: leave the app open for a minute until the queue is empty.

**Don't** clear your browser data or sign out while records are pending.
